# Art reference board

Links with attribution only. No third-party image is stored in this repository (SPEC.md section 4.1). The references inform rendering craft, proportions and obstacles. Nothing is copied.

## Rendering craft

- Primary: [Unusual Findings](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1605320/Unusual_Findings/) by Epic Llama Games. Clusters, texture density and character readability.
- Secondary: [Tales of the Neon Sea](https://www.thermitegames.com/games/tales-of-the-neon-sea/) by Palm Pioneer, and [Forge of the Fae](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2160070/).
- Composition and detail: [Kingdom Two Crowns](https://kingdomthegame.com/kingdom-two-crowns/) and [Eastward](https://chucklefish.org/blog/introducing-eastward-by-pixpil/) by Pixpil.

## Course furniture

- Owner's reference photos, shared in the project conversation on 2026-11-18 and not stored here: a race hill with a wide flight of stairs cut into the bank, banners along both sides of the stairs and a ridable mud chute beside it; and a race flyover with steel handrails, dark deck plates and sponsor banners on its sides.
- [Cyclo-cross Structures](https://usacycling.org/event-organizer/toolkit/operations/event-safety/course-design/cyclocross), USA Cycling: flyovers need handrails, enough length on top to remount, and non-slip strips on the deck; run-ups have stairs on the way up and a ramp on the way down.
- [Photo report from the Tábor World Cup 2022](https://www.welovecycling.com/cs/2022/10/23/paradni-generalka-nadseni-fanousci-medailovy-uspech-ceskeho-reprezentanta-usmevy-rozdavajici-svetova-hvezda-zdenek-stybar-svetovy-pohar-v-cyklokrosu-v-tabore-byl-predposledni-rijnovou-nedeli-skv/), We Love Cycling: stairs and barriers with the crowd close to the course. The photos could not be opened from the build environment.
