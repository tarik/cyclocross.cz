# Art reference board

Links with attribution only. No third-party image is stored in this repository (SPEC.md section 4.1). The references inform rendering craft, proportions and obstacles. Nothing is copied.

## Rendering craft

- Primary: [Unusual Findings](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1605320/Unusual_Findings/) by Epic Llama Games. Clusters, texture density and character readability.
- Secondary: [Tales of the Neon Sea](https://www.thermitegames.com/games/tales-of-the-neon-sea/) by Palm Pioneer, and [Forge of the Fae](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2160070/).
- Composition and detail: [Kingdom Two Crowns](https://kingdomthegame.com/kingdom-two-crowns/) and [Eastward](https://chucklefish.org/blog/introducing-eastward-by-pixpil/) by Pixpil.
