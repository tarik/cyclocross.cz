---

Is a race missing from the calendar, or has a date or venue changed? We verify every change against an official source, so please include a link.

The quickest way is through the forms on GitHub:

- [Suggest a race](https://github.com/tarik/cyclocross.cz/issues/new?template=add-event.yml)
- [Report a correction](https://github.com/tarik/cyclocross.cz/issues/new?template=correct-event.yml)
- [Report a site problem](https://github.com/tarik/cyclocross.cz/issues/new?template=site-problem.yml)

The forms are public, so do not put private details in them. A contact route outside GitHub will be added.
